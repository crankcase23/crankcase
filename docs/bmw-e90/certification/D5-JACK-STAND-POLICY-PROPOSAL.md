# D5: one jack-stand policy for the 2010 BMW 328i guides (PROPOSAL, nothing changed yet)

Status: proposed 2026-10-12, waiting for Boss. No guide text was changed for D5. No stand location is published anywhere in this proposal.

## What the evidence supports

- BMW's owner's manual (2011 E90 text copy) says the jacking points are "at the positions shown in the illustration". The illustration could not be read, so no stand position comes from BMW.
- The car has four side jacking points (plastic pads at the sills). One forum archive reports the pads can crush under a floor-jack cup and the blocks can detach. One thread quotes BMW that centre jacking at the rear is not allowed on the E90; sources conflict on the front centre point.
- No source read says where a jack **stand** goes. Today's guides say three different things (below).

## What the guides say today (the contradiction)

| Guide | Line today |
|---|---|
| 1 Front Brake Pads, 5 Rear Brake Pads | Step and safety line: stands "at the side jacking points" (the brake guides describe the pad positions) |
| 13 Tire Rotation | "The correct stand position for the E90 sedan is not given in any source read"; use a shop lift if no safe point is found |
| 16 Oil Change | Stands "at the four side points", pad adapter recommended, a front-centre jack point "also reported"; ramps allowed |
| 2, 4, 6, 8, 15 | Generic: "support the car on stands or a lift, never on a jack alone" |

## Proposed single policy (wording for every guide that lifts the car)

> Never work under, or take a wheel off, a car held only by a jack. Support it on rated jack stands on hard, level ground, or use a lift or drive-on ramps where the job allows. Use the jacking points shown in the jacking illustration in your owner's manual, with a pad or adapter between the jack and the plastic pad. If you cannot identify a safe place for the stand from your manual, stop and use a shop lift.

What this policy deliberately does **not** do:

- It does not name a stand position, a stand adapter type, the pinch weld, or any frame point. Those are unverified and stay unpublished until a BMW document (owner's manual illustration or TIS) is read.
- It does not repeat "at the side jacking points" for stands. The side points are published only as the jack's lift points, per the owner's manual.
- It does not allow the differential cover or either centre point.

## What would change if Boss approves

1. Guides 1, 5 and 16: replace the stand wording with the policy sentence above (the lift-point description of the plastic side pads stays, since the manual confirms the points exist).
2. Guide 13: keep its stop-and-use-a-lift fallback, aligned to the same sentence.
3. Guides 2, 4, 6, 8, 15: replace the generic line with the same sentence.
4. One regression test: no BMW guide may tell the owner to place a stand at a named location, and every guide that lifts the car must carry the policy sentence.

## Decision for Boss

Approve this wording (or edit it), and say whether "use the owner's manual illustration, otherwise a shop lift" is acceptable to put in front of customers. Until then the guides keep their current wording and Rear Brake Pads stays restricted.
