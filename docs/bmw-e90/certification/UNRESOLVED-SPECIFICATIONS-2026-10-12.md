# Unresolved specifications, 2010 BMW 328i (D1 record, 2026-10-12)

Result of a second hunt for BMW primary documents (about 36 searches, 40 pages). **No BMW-hosted or NHTSA-hosted document gave a torque or capacity for this car.** Nothing below is certified and none of it was guessed. All pages were read through a summarizing fetch tool, so every value is a paraphrase; BMW TIS pages found are copies hosted on forum sites.

Status words: **COPY OF BMW PAGE** = a reproduced BMW TIS or service bulletin page on a third-party host; **SECONDARY** = 2+ independent websites; **SINGLE** = one source; **CONFLICT** = sources disagree; **NOT FOUND**.

1. **Boss, 10 minutes on the car and the glovebox manual:** photograph the owner's manual Technical Data page (capacities) and the jacking page; read the trunk battery label; note the rear guide-screw head type and size.
2. **A BMW source for one sitting:** BMW's tech-info site sells time-limited TIS access (cost and availability not checked; this needs Boss's approval and his own login), or a Bentley E90 manual, or an independent BMW tech reading the items above off ISTA.
3. **Paste links in chat:** the fetch tool only opens links that appear in a user message. If Boss pastes the links he trusts (for example the BMW training-document index or any TIS copy), those pages can be read directly.
