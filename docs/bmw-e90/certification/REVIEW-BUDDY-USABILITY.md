# Usability and $20 value review, 2010 BMW 328i guides

You are the customer. You do not need to know BMWs. Use a phone (that is where this gets used, under a car, with dirty hands). About 30 minutes.

## What you are testing

Sixteen how-to guides for one car, a 2010 BMW 328i sedan (automatic, rear-wheel drive): front brake pads, fuse and bulb, key fob battery, oxygen sensor, rear brake pads, serpentine belt, battery, coolant, fluid checks, cabin air filter, PCV inspection, engine air filter, tire rotation, wiper blades, differential fluid, oil change.
Every guide is clearly marked as an incomplete draft that is not yet checked against BMW service data. That is on purpose. A few steps are deliberately "blocked": the app tells you to stop and see a shop instead of showing a risky procedure. That is also on purpose. Tell us if a blocked step is confusing, not that it is blocked.

## Do this

1. Sign in with the account the owner gives you, add the 2010 BMW 328i, and confirm "Automatic" and "Standard RWD".
2. Open **Oil Change**. Read the Planning view. Then start **Wrench Mode** and go through every step on your phone. Use Back at least once.
3. Open **Wiper Blades** (easy) and **Front Brake Pads** (hard). Planning view only for brake pads, then Wrench Mode for the first six steps.
4. Open **Fuse & Bulb** and find the two blocked steps. Read what they tell you.
5. Open **Edit Configuration**, change the transmission to "Not sure", and open **Oil Change** again. What changed? Was it clear why?
6. Put it back and finish the oil change walkthrough to the end (the completion screen).
