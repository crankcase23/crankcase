# BMW 328i (E90) — what only the owner can supply

Ranked by how many guides each item unblocks. Per-guide detail is in `EVIDENCE-SUMMARY.md`.

## One lookup that settles most configuration questions
1. **Factory option list for VIN WBAPH7C58AA175760** (BMW dealer, or a build-data lookup). It settles engine code, drive type, transmission, headlight type, key type (Comfort Access or not), wheel and tire package and brake package in one go.

## Photos of the car (also usable as image evidence, rights are the owner's)
2. Emissions label under the hood (N52 versus N51), and the top of the engine with the filter housing cap and oil filler cap visible. Say whether a plastic engine cover is fitted.
3. The underside near the front-centre with the undertray hatch open, drain plug visible.
4. Battery label in the trunk (Ah, size, AGM or not).
5. Both keys, front and back.
6. Old fog bulb markings, and the length of the passenger wiper blade.
7. Door-jamb sticker (build date and tire placard).
8. Numbers printed on the old belt and tensioner when they next come off.

## Documents
9. Owner's manual pages: Capacities, Lamps & Bulbs, Engine oil approval. One BMW primary document would move many single-source facts to verified. Access to BMW TIS or ETK for this VIN would be better still.
